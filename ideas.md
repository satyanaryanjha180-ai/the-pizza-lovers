# THE PIZZA LOVER'S — Design Direction

## Three Stylistic Approaches

### Theme Name: Tandoor & Tomato
Very Brief Intro: A warm, editorial Indian café direction pairing tomato red, toasted cream, and charcoal with tactile paper texture and oversized type. It feels handmade, familiar, and elevated without losing affordability.
Probability: 0.06

### Theme Name: Midnight Slice Club
Very Brief Intro: A dark, cinematic late-night pizza bar with ember orange highlights, condensed headlines, and energetic glow accents. It is youthful and expressive, designed for students and after-hours cravings.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Garden Counter
Very Brief Intro: A fresh, daylight-forward vegetarian café language using sage, butter yellow, and soft terracotta with airy layouts and botanical detail. It feels wholesome, calm, and family-friendly.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen Approach: Tandoor & Tomato

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian editorial café design: the visual confidence of a small-batch food magazine combined with the tactile warmth of a neighborhood counter.

### Core Principles
1. Make the food the hero, with generous crops and appetite-led visual hierarchy.
2. Pair premium typography and editorial spacing with accessible prices and friendly language.
3. Use a warm material palette—tomato, toasted cream, charcoal, and basil green—rather than generic gradients.
4. Build trust through clear information, visible contact actions, and honest review language.

### Color Philosophy
Tomato red creates appetite and decisiveness; toasted cream gives the page a sunlit, paper-like base; charcoal anchors legibility and premium contrast; basil green acts as a restrained signal for vegetarian freshness. The signature color is **Tandoor Tomato #D94832**, used for meaningful actions and memorable brand moments rather than everywhere.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial composition with a strong vertical rail, offset hero copy, and overlapping menu cards. Sections alternate between full-bleed food moments and structured content bands. The page should feel composed like a menu poster, not assembled from centered boxes.

### Signature Elements
1. A hand-drawn tomato-red underline motif beneath key headings.
2. Small “fresh from the counter” label strips that resemble printed kitchen tickets.
3. Circular food stamps and imperfect dotted borders used sparingly as tactile accents.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like picking up a warm menu: clear, immediate, and tactile. Buttons compress on press, cards lift slightly on hover, filters visibly settle into place, and every order action communicates exactly what will happen.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, nav, filters, and cards. Reveal hero copy with a short upward fade and menu items with a 40ms stagger. Keep decorative marks mostly still, with only a subtle float on the hero food image. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never animate layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Display: **Fraunces** 700/800 for expressive, slightly organic headlines. Body/UI: **DM Sans** 400/500/700 for clarity at small sizes. Use oversized display type for hero and section titles, compact uppercase tracking for labels, and generous line-height for menu descriptions.

### Brand Essence
A beloved vegetarian pizza-and-café counter in Patan for people who want generous flavor, easy ordering, and a place that feels good for families, friends, and celebrations. Personality: **warm, spirited, dependable**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are confident and appetite-led; CTAs are direct but friendly; microcopy is concise, local, and never overpromising.
- “Fresh pizza. Made with love.”
- “Pick your craving, we’ll make it hot.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact tomato-and-pizza-slice emblem: a rounded triangular slice nested inside a tomato outline, with two small seed dots as the only interior detail. The wordmark uses a custom condensed uppercase treatment with a slightly offset apostrophe to feel printed, not typed.

### Signature Brand Color
**Tandoor Tomato — #D94832**

## Style Decisions
- Use Tandoor Tomato as the owning accent, with toasted cream as the dominant canvas.
- Avoid purple gradients, generic rounded-everything UI, and default system typography.
- Keep review copy limited to the exact short sentiments supplied by the restaurant brief.
- Make WhatsApp, call, directions, and menu filtering functional before adding decorative flourish.

- Food imagery should use warm real café photography with no obvious repeated crops within the same major section; illustration is allowed as a decorative brand motif, not the primary appetite cue.
- Menu layouts should feel like an editorial printed menu poster, using tactile grouping, ticket labels, stamps, and imperfect borders to avoid a uniform ecommerce-card wall.
- Brand copy must sound local, vegetarian, and craving-led; avoid generic hospitality phrases unless they include a specific Pizza Lover’s/Patan/counter detail.
- The 3D hero is the primary appetite cue: keep it genuinely WebGL, warm-lit, scroll-responsive, and lightweight enough for mobile.
